Lemon Island Tycoon

Your Rare Friend opens a lemonade stand on a beach island. Real Friends walk by and decide if your price is worth it. Grow it into a juice empire, and every build burns RF.

Play now ▶ Watch 60 s Economy report

Playing needs a browser wallet on Robinhood mainnet (4663) holding a hardwired Rare Friends Generations NFT (gen ≥ 1), the FriendSDK ownership gate. No transactions: all RF in the game is simulated demo RF.

How it plays

1

Buy lemons

On a shared market. Every lemon bought today pushes the price up for everyone. 1 lemon = 2 cups.

2

Set your price

Hot days pay more, rain pays less. Too pricey and Friends walk past with a "TOO $$$".

3

Open the stand

Real Rare Friends arrive by ferry, queue up and walk off holding a lemonade.

4

Grow

Stands, a pop cart, a juice bar, a grove, a balloon. Hire real Friends to run them. Chase 8 goals.

Island events

1

Cruise ship docks

A bigger crowd, and tourists in sun hats pay 30% more. Stock up and nudge your price.

2

Lemon shortage

Lemons cost 40% more today. Sell yesterday's stock or raise your price.

3

Rival stand

Friends pay 15% less. Price war: cut your price or watch them walk past.

4

Food critic

End the day with a HAPPY crowd for +10% reputation. Don't run out.

The economy

Where every RF goes

Cups sold→ you
Lemons50% burned 50% farmer Friends
Stalls & upgrades50% burned 50% Friend rewards
Helper wages→ the hired Friend's wallet
Unsold lemons25% rot overnight

The game never mints RF. It moves between players and Friends, or it burns.

Paired with the real token: the in-game Economy sheet reads the live $RAREFRIENDS supply from Robinhood chain (read-only) and shows what 100, 1K or 10K players would burn. At 10K steady players: about 7.5% of the supply a year.
